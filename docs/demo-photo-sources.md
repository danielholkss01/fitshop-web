# Homepage inspiration photos

The homepage uses three photographs from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/). They are general fashion inspiration, not photographs of the sample items in an outfit card. Fit&Shop does not imply that the pictured people endorse the service or that a photographed item will fit a shopper.

The outfit cards use separate sketches for each top, bottom, shoe and optional accessory. This avoids presenting unrelated people and garments as one coordinated outfit. Sample names, sizes and prices are illustrative. Actual partner listings should use approved photographs of the exact linked product.
